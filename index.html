Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Solidus ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Solidus is developed and published by Famz Tech.

1. Information We Collect

Solidus is designed with privacy in mind. Most of your data — expenses, categories, budgets, and settings — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

  • Expense Data: Amounts, categories, dates, notes, and optional receipt photos you enter are stored locally on your device using on-device storage.
  • Receipt Photos: If you attach a receipt photo, it is processed on-device (including any automatic amount detection) and stored locally. Photos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Backup Data: If you use the export/import backup feature, your data is saved to a file you choose to share (e.g., via your device's share sheet, email, or cloud storage of your choice). We do not have access to these files.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display ads within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please see Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this data.
  • Notification Preferences: If you enable reminders, your notification preferences and scheduling are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

Since your expense data stays on your device, we do not use it for any purpose beyond powering the App's features for you. Advertising data collected by AdMob is used to display ads and is governed by Google's own privacy practices.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal expense data with third parties. The only third-party service integrated into the App is Google AdMob, used solely for displaying advertisements.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your expense data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information will be permanently deleted unless you have created a backup export.

5. Your Choices

  • You can delete individual expenses or all data at any time from within the App.
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the App's settings or your device's system settings.
  • You can control ad personalization through your device's advertising settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Solidus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at zarnoors@gmail.com.
